Privacy

Last updated 2 Nov 2026

This site is run by TechieJack. You can use the contact form to ask about anything on this page.

What I measure

This site uses its own analytics, which I run myself on my own server. For each visit it records:

The page you opened and when, roughly how long the page was open and visible, which links and buttons you clicked, and your approximate location (country and region, and sometimes city).

What I do not do

The analytics does not use cookies or store anything on your device. It does not record your IP address, your name, or anything you type into the contact form. The contact form's spam protection is separate and is described below. It does not try to recognise you when you come back, so each visit counts as a new one.

If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is recorded at all.

Why and for how long

I use this to understand how the site is used, because I have a legitimate interest in knowing that. Visit records are deleted automatically after 180 days. I do not sell them or share them for advertising.

Location and other services

Analytics and contact form requests pass through Cloudflare. For analytics, Cloudflare works out your approximate location from your IP address and passes only that location to my server. The company that hosts this site may also process your IP address to deliver its pages.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are saved on my own server and sent to my private inbox, which is run by an email provider, so I can read and reply to them. I do not use them for anything else or share them with anyone else. If I reply, you will see the email address I reply from. Your IP address is used briefly to limit spam and is not saved with your message. Copies on my server are deleted automatically after 12 months, or sooner if I delete them. Copies in my inbox are kept for as long as I need them for our conversation.

Spam protection

The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to stop automated spam. When the form comes into view, your browser loads Turnstile from Cloudflare, which receives your IP address and technical details about your browser and device so it can tell people from bots. My server also sends Cloudflare your IP address when it checks the result. Cloudflare handles this data under its own Turnstile privacy policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to your data, or to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because analytics visits are not linked to your identity, I usually cannot find a specific person's visits, but I can do this for any message you have sent me.
